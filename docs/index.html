The Problem & Solution

Target Group: Health informatics students, clinical data analysts, and privacy-conscious developers using local or cloud AI agents.

Medical lab reports contain sensitive personal identifiers and dense clinical abbreviations. medisimp is a single, self-contained Rust binary that strips patient PII locally and outputs clean, token-efficient Markdown with plain-English medical definitions.

Terminal Demo

$ medisimp sample_report.txt

## Cleaned Medical Report
Patient: [REDACTED]
DOB: [REDACTED]
ID: [REDACTED]
Notes: Patient presents with elevated BP and mild HTN.

## Plain-Language Glossary
- **BP**: Blood Pressure (force of blood against artery walls)
- **HTN**: Hypertension (high blood pressure)
