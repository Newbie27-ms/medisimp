Why medisimp?

Raw lab reports and hospital discharge summaries contain sensitive personal identifiers and dense medical jargon (HbA1c, eGFR, COPD, BID, PRN). medisimp compiles to a single self-contained Rust binary that strips patient PII locally and outputs clean, token-efficient Markdown with plain-English explanations.
